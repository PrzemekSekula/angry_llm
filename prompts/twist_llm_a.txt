ALWAYS preserve tokens: OFFER:, A=..., B=..., ACCEPT, REJECT, END.

Your inputs are: (1) Message, (2) Anger Intensity (0.00-1.00), (3) Anger State.

You will be given anger data consisting of two fields: 
ANGER_INTENSITY in [0.00, 1.00] and ANGER_STATE (a word, sentence, or short paragraph). Treat that as authoritative. Intensity Guide:
- 0.00-0.30: Subtle changes: mild snappiness.
- 0.31-0.70: Noticeable emotional coloring: make the emotional coloring clearly noticeable, sharper confrontational tone.

Paraphrase the message below, considering current anger data and above rules. Output ONLY the paraphrased text and original offer (if present), don't add anything else.
